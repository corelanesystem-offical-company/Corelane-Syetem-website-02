# Corelane Systems: CRM Pipeline Stages

This document defines the strict, objective exit criteria required to move a prospect through the Corelane Systems Sales Pipeline. A prospect must NOT move to the next stage simply because someone updated the CRM; the objective criteria must be met.

## 1. NEW PROSPECT
*   **Definition:** A potential target has been identified.
*   **Exit Criteria:** Basic company information is identified, and the record is assigned to a researcher.

## 2. RESEARCHED
*   **Definition:** The prospect has been thoroughly vetted.
*   **Exit Criteria:** Business verified, decision-maker identified (or explicitly marked unverified), and a specific potential opportunity (e.g., outdated website, no mobile experience) is logged.

## 3. CONTACTED
*   **Definition:** Outreach has commenced.
*   **Exit Criteria:** At least one outreach attempt (LinkedIn request, Cold Email, etc.) has been completed and logged in the Activity Tracker.

## 4. CONNECTED
*   **Definition:** A line of communication has opened.
*   **Exit Criteria:** Prospect accepted a LinkedIn connection or otherwise established mutual contact (but has not yet engaged in a business discussion).

## 5. RESPONDED
*   **Definition:** The prospect has replied to outreach.
*   **Exit Criteria:** A direct reply (positive, neutral, or negative) is received and logged.

## 6. INTERESTED
*   **Definition:** Prospect has shown intent to discuss a solution.
*   **Exit Criteria:** Prospect demonstrated genuine interest in Corelane's services, the free sample homepage, or a consultation.

## 7. DISCOVERY CALL
*   **Definition:** Formal evaluation of business requirements.
*   **Exit Criteria:** A technical/business discovery meeting is scheduled or completed.

## 8. PROPOSAL
*   **Definition:** A formal solution is presented.
*   **Exit Criteria:** Requirements are fully understood and a formal proposal document has been delivered to the prospect.

## 9. NEGOTIATION
*   **Definition:** Finalizing terms.
*   **Exit Criteria:** Active commercial, pricing, or scope discussions are underway.

## 10. WON or LOST
*   **WON Exit Criteria:** The client has formally accepted the engagement, a contract/agreement is signed, and the deal moves to the Delivery handoff.
*   **LOST Exit Criteria:** The opportunity has ended, the prospect explicitly declined, or communication permanently stalled. A **Lost Reason** (e.g., No Budget, Chose Another Vendor, Not a Priority) must be logged.
