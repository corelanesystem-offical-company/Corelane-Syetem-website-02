# Corelane Systems: Client Acquisition & Sales Playbook

This playbook defines the standardized sales process for Corelane Systems. It is designed to acquire high-value B2B clients in Pakistan, UAE, UK, USA, and Canada through personalized, high-quality, low-pressure outreach.

## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
Corelane Systems targets businesses that need to solve operational or growth challenges using technology (websites, custom software, cloud engineering, business automation).
*   **Company Size:** SMEs, Startups, and growing professional businesses.
*   **Geographies:** Primary (Pakistan), Secondary (UAE, UK, USA, Canada).
*   **Mindset:** Looking for an engineering partner, not just a cheap freelancer.

## 2. Target Industries
1. Law Firms
2. Real Estate
3. Clinics / Healthcare
4. Restaurants
5. Professional Services
6. Architecture / Construction
7. E-commerce
8. SMEs
9. Startups

## 3. Target Countries & Strategy
*   **Pakistan (Local Focus):** Prioritize specific commercial areas in Karachi (Clifton, DHA, PECHS, Gulshan, Gulistan-e-Jauhar, North Nazimabad, Bahadurabad, Shahrah-e-Faisal, Saddar, Korangi).
*   **International (UAE, UK, USA, Canada):** Adjust messaging to account for business culture, time zones, and communication style. Direct international prospects to the corresponding `/locations/` page, not the homepage.

## 4. Prospect Research
For each prospect, collect ONLY verified information. Do not invent details.
*   **Sources:** Google Maps, LinkedIn, Company Websites, Industry Directories.
*   **Website Status Classification:**
    *   NO WEBSITE
    *   Website exists but outdated
    *   Website exists but poor mobile experience
    *   Website exists but weak conversion
    *   Website exists and appears modern
*   **Decision Makers:** Owner, Founder, MD, Partner, CEO, Director, CTO, Head of IT. If unknown, mark as "Decision maker not verified".

## 5. Qualification & Lead Scoring
Not every prospect is a qualified lead.
*   **A — HIGH PRIORITY:** Strong opportunity + identifiable decision maker + public contact information.
*   **B — MEDIUM PRIORITY:** Potential opportunity but weaker information.
*   **C — LOW PRIORITY:** Limited information or weak fit.

## 6. Outreach Strategy (Multi-Channel)
Use LinkedIn, Email, WhatsApp, Phone, and Contact Forms where publicly available and appropriate.
**Rules:**
*   NO SPAM.
*   NO mass-sending identical messages.
*   Keep emails to 80–130 words.
*   Include at least ONE verified observation per prospect.
*   Link to the specific `/industries/` or `/locations/` page when relevant.

## 7. Follow-up Sequence
*   **DAY 1:** Research + LinkedIn connection (No hard pitch).
*   **DAY 3:** Short personalized LinkedIn message.
*   **DAY 5:** Personalized email.
*   **DAY 8:** LinkedIn follow-up.
*   **DAY 11:** Email follow-up with useful observation.
*   **DAY 15:** Final polite follow-up.
*(Stop contacting after this sequence unless they engage).*

## 8. Free Homepage Sample Strategy
Offer a limited number of qualified prospects a *Free Sample Homepage Concept*.
*   **Process:** Prospect identified → Research → Personalized outreach → Prospect interested → Create homepage concept → Send preview → Discovery conversation → Proposal.
*   **Positioning:** "I put together a small homepage concept to demonstrate one possible direction for your online presence." (Do NOT say their current website is bad).

## 10. KPIs (Key Performance Indicators)
Focus on conversations, not just volume. Target: 50 prospects/week initially.
*   Prospects researched
*   Messages / Emails sent
*   LinkedIn requests & Connections accepted
*   Replies (Positive replies)
*   Discovery calls
*   Proposals & Won/Lost deals
*   Conversion rate & Average deal value

## 11. Sales Handoff Responsibilities
*   **Researcher:** Finds verified information.
*   **Sales:** Personalizes outreach.
*   **Designer:** Creates sample homepage (only for qualified prospects).
*   **Developer:** Supports technical questions.
*   **Founder / Team Lead:** Handles discovery and proposals.

## 12. Discovery Call Framework
Use these questions to guide the conversation:
1. Tell us about your business.
2. What are you trying to improve?
3. What is the current system?
4. What challenges are you experiencing?
5. What would success look like?
6. Who are the users/customers?
7. What integrations are required?
8. What is your preferred timeline?
9. Who is involved in the decision?
10. Is there an approximate budget range? *(Do not force budget early)*.

## 13. Proposal Process
Structure proposals professionally:
1. Executive Summary
2. Business Understanding & Current Situation
3. Proposed Solution & Scope
4. Technology & Architecture
5. Timeline & Deliverables
6. Investment
7. Assumptions & Exclusions
8. Next Steps
