# Corelane Systems: CRM & Lead Fields

This document outlines the standard fields to be used in the Corelane Systems CRM (Customer Relationship Management) system to track prospects and leads efficiently.

## Prospect Research Fields
For every prospect, researchers must collect ONLY verified information.

*   **Company Name:** (Required)
*   **Industry:** (Required)
*   **Country:** (Required)
*   **City:** (Required)
*   **Website:** (Required)
*   **Website Status:** 
    *   NO WEBSITE
    *   Website exists but outdated
    *   Website exists but poor mobile experience
    *   Website exists but weak conversion
    *   Website exists and appears modern
*   **Google Maps URL:** (Optional)
*   **LinkedIn:** (Optional)
*   **Instagram:** (Optional)
*   **Facebook:** (Optional)
*   **Public Email:** (Optional)
*   **Public Phone:** (Optional)
*   **Decision Maker:** (If unknown: "Decision maker not verified")
*   **Decision Maker Role:** (e.g., Owner, CEO, CTO, IT Manager)
*   **Business Observation:** (Verified detail to use in outreach)
*   **Potential Opportunity:** (e.g., Lead generation, mobile optimization)
*   **Source:** (Where the prospect was found)
*   **Date Researched:** (Required)

## Core CRM Pipeline Fields

*   **Lead ID:** Unique identifier.
*   **Company:** Linked to Prospect Research.
*   **Industry:** Linked to Prospect Research.
*   **Country:** Linked to Prospect Research.
*   **City:** Linked to Prospect Research.
*   **Website:** Linked to Prospect Research.
*   **Decision Maker:** Linked to Prospect Research.
*   **Role:** Linked to Prospect Research.
*   **Email:** Contact email.
*   **Phone:** Contact phone.
*   **LinkedIn:** Prospect/Company profile.
*   **WhatsApp:** If applicable.
*   **Source:** Linked to Prospect Research.

### Qualification & Stage Fields

*   **Lead Score:** 
    *   `A — HIGH PRIORITY`: Strong opportunity + identifiable decision maker + public contact information.
    *   `B — MEDIUM PRIORITY`: Potential opportunity but weaker information.
    *   `C — LOW PRIORITY`: Limited information or weak fit.
*   **Lead Status:** 
    *   `HOT`
    *   `WARM`
    *   `EXPLORING`
    *   `NOT QUALIFIED`
    *   *(Note: Do not confuse lead score with sales stage).*
*   **Sales Stage:** 
    *   `NEW PROSPECT`
    *   `RESEARCHED`
    *   `CONTACTED`
    *   `CONNECTED`
    *   `RESPONDED`
    *   `INTERESTED`
    *   `DISCOVERY CALL`
    *   `PROPOSAL`
    *   `NEGOTIATION`
    *   `WON`
    *   `LOST`
*   **First Contact:** Date of first outreach.
*   **Last Contact:** Date of most recent communication.
*   **Next Follow-up:** Date for the next scheduled action.
*   **Service Interest:** (e.g., Website Development, Web App, Cloud Engineering, Custom Software, DevOps).
*   **Opportunity:** Summary of the proposed solution.
*   **Notes:** Running log of communications and observations.
*   **Proposal Value:** Estimated or finalized deal value.
*   **Outcome:** Reason for Won/Lost status.
