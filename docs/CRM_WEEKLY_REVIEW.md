# Corelane Systems: CRM Weekly Pipeline Review

Pipeline hygiene is critical for a functioning B2B sales operation. This document outlines the mandatory weekly review process.

## Pipeline Hygiene Rules
Before the review, the Sales Owner must ensure:
1. No duplicate company or contact records exist.
2. No fake or assumed information exists (update with verified data).
3. No active opportunities exist without a **Next Action** and **Next Follow-up Date**.
4. No closed/lost opportunities remain in active stages.

## Weekly Review Questions
The sales team must answer the following 10 questions every week:

1. **Which new prospects were added?**
   *Review the quality of research and ensure target industries are met.*

2. **Which prospects were contacted?**
   *Verify that the outreach limit (e.g., 30 highly personalized messages) was hit without resorting to spam.*

3. **Which prospects responded?**
   *Analyze the nature of the responses. Were they positive, negative, or neutral?*

4. **Which leads became qualified?**
   *Review prospects moving from `RESEARCHED` to higher stages. Validate Lead Scores.*

5. **Which discovery calls happened?**
   *Review notes from the calls. Were business requirements clearly gathered?*

6. **Which proposals were sent?**
   *Verify that proposals match the client's needs and fall within their expected budget/timeline.*

7. **Which deals are stalled?**
   *Identify prospects in `PROPOSAL` or `NEGOTIATION` for too long. What is blocking the deal?*

8. **Which follow-ups are overdue?**
   *Check the CRM for missed `Next Follow-up Date` triggers. Action them immediately.*

9. **Which opportunities should be closed-lost?**
   *Move unresponsive or unqualified leads out of the active pipeline. Log the `Lost Reason`.*

10. **What is the next action for every active opportunity?**
    *Confirm that no lead is "floating" without a clear path forward.*

## Stale Lead Protocol
During the review, address leads flagged for inactivity:
*   **7 Days:** Ensure a follow-up action is scheduled.
*   **14 Days:** Attempt final outreach (Day 15 sequence).
*   **30 Days:** If unresponsive, DO NOT delete. Move the prospect to `NURTURE` (with a Nurture Reason and Next Review Date) or mark as `LOST` (Lost Reason: No Response).
