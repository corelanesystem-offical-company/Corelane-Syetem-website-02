# Corelane Systems: CRM Sales Dashboard

This document defines the metrics and views required to monitor the health of the Corelane Systems sales pipeline. These dashboards focus on quality and conversion over raw volume.

## Recommended CRM Views
Configure the CRM (HubSpot, Google Sheets, etc.) with these specific saved views:

### Pipeline Management
1. **All Prospects:** The complete database.
2. **New Prospects:** Leads awaiting research.
3. **Research Required:** Information verification pending.
4. **Contacted:** Outreach sequence initiated.
5. **Awaiting Response:** Ball is in the prospect's court.
6. **Follow-ups Due Today:** Critical daily task list.
7. **Follow-ups Due This Week:** Weekly planning list.

### Active Deal Management
8. **Hot Leads:** Filtered by Lead Status = `HOT`.
9. **Discovery Calls:** Prospects in the `DISCOVERY CALL` stage.
10. **Proposals:** Prospects in the `PROPOSAL` stage.
11. **Negotiations:** Prospects in the `NEGOTIATION` stage.

### Outcomes
12. **Won:** Successfully closed deals.
13. **Lost:** Filtered by Lost Reason for analysis.

### Geographic Segmentation
14. **Pakistan:** Local target prospects.
15. **UAE:** International prospects.
16. **UK:** International prospects.
17. **USA:** International prospects.
18. **Canada:** International prospects.

## Daily Sales Dashboard
A high-level view for daily operations and task management.

*   **New Prospects:** Number of targets added today.
*   **Research Completed:** Number of prospects verified today.
*   **Outreach Sent:** Total personalized messages/emails sent today.
*   **Replies:** Total responses received today.
*   **Positive Replies:** Responses showing interest.
*   **Follow-ups Due:** Tasks requiring action today.
*   **Discovery Calls:** Meetings scheduled or held today.
*   **Proposals:** Proposals sent today.
*   **Won Deals:** Deals closed today.

---

## Weekly Sales Dashboard
A strategic view for the Weekly Pipeline Review.

*   **Total Prospects:** Entire database size.
*   **New Prospects:** Targets added this week.
*   **Qualified Prospects:** Prospects passing research filters.
*   **Emails Sent:** Volume of initial and follow-up emails.
*   **LinkedIn Messages:** Volume of LinkedIn outreach.
*   **WhatsApp Conversations:** Volume of direct chat outreach.
*   **Replies / Positive Replies:** Conversion from outreach to conversation.
*   **Discovery Calls:** Quality metric.
*   **Proposals:** Bottom-of-funnel volume.
*   **Won / Lost Deals:** Outcomes.
*   **Pipeline Value:** Total estimated value of active proposals/negotiations.
*   **Revenue Won:** Actual contracted value this week.

---

## Important Performance Metrics
Do NOT focus purely on "Messages Sent." Optimize the system using these conversion metrics:

*   **Positive Reply Rate:** (Positive Replies / Total Outreach)
*   **Discovery Call Rate:** (Discovery Calls / Prospects Contacted)
*   **Proposal Rate:** (Proposals Sent / Discovery Calls Held)
*   **Win Rate:** (Won Deals / Total Proposals Sent)
*   **Average Deal Value:** (Total Revenue Won / Number of Won Deals)
*   **Sales Cycle Length:** Average days from First Contact to WON.
*   **Pipeline Value:** Potential revenue in active stages.
*   **Revenue Won:** Confirmed revenue.
