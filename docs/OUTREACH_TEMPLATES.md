# Corelane Systems: Outreach Templates

These templates are starting points. **Every message must be personalized** with at least one verified observation about the prospect's business.

## Rules for Outreach
*   Do NOT use generic openings like "Dear Sir/Madam" or "I hope this email finds you well."
*   Do NOT use "We are the leading company..." unless specifically supported.
*   Keep emails to 80–130 words.
*   Avoid spammy subjects: NO "URGENT", "LIMITED OFFER", "ACT NOW", or "FREE!!!".

---

## 1. LinkedIn Connection Request
*Limit: 300 characters.*

**Template:**
Hi [Name],

I came across [Company] while researching [industry] businesses in [location]. I work with Corelane Systems in software and cloud engineering. Would be glad to connect.

---

## 2. LinkedIn First Message (Day 3)
*Sent after connection is accepted.*

**Template:**
Hi [Name],

Thanks for connecting.

I was looking into [Company] and noticed [specific verified observation]. 

We work with businesses on websites, web applications and cloud/DevOps solutions. 

If technology improvements are on your roadmap, happy to share a few ideas.

---

## 3. Cold Email (Day 5)

**Subject Examples:**
*   A quick idea for [Company]
*   A website idea for [Company]
*   Digital opportunity for [Company]
*   Question about [Company]

**Template:**
Hi [Name],

I came across [Company] while researching [industry] businesses in [location].

I noticed [verified observation]. 

There may be an opportunity to improve [specific business experience], particularly around [website / lead generation / customer experience].

Corelane Systems helps businesses build professional websites, web applications and cloud-based solutions. 

I put together a simple idea for how this could look for [Company]. 

Would you be open to me sharing it?

Best,  
Muhammad Zaid  
Corelane Systems  

*(Optionally link to the specific Industry or Location landing page, e.g., `/industries/law-firms` or `/locations/uae`)*

---

## 4. WhatsApp Outreach
*Use ONLY when appropriate and publicly available. Do not send unsolicited long sales messages.*

**Template:**
Assalam-o-Alaikum [Name],

I'm Zaid from Corelane Systems.

I came across [Company] while researching businesses in [area]. 

We work on professional websites and web applications for businesses. 

I also prepared a small homepage concept for [Company]. 

If you'd like, I can send it here for you to review. 

Thank you.

---

## 5. Follow-up 1 (Email / LinkedIn - Day 11)

**Template:**
Hi [Name],

Just following up on my previous message. 

I had prepared a small idea around [specific opportunity] for [Company]. 

Happy to send it over if it would be useful.

---

## 6. Follow-up 2 (Email / LinkedIn - Day 15)
*Final polite follow-up. Stop contacting after this message unless they engage.*

**Template:**
Hi [Name],

I'll keep this brief. 

If improving [website / digital presence / application] isn't a priority right now, no problem at all.

If it becomes relevant later, I'd be happy to help.

Best,  
Zaid
